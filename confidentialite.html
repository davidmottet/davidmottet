Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 22, 2026

Forge Shorts is a private tool operated by David Mottet (individual developer) to publish his own videos to his own YouTube channels. It has no other users and is not distributed.

YouTube API Services

Forge Shorts uses YouTube API Services. By using it, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service: https://www.youtube.com/t/terms

Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

What it accesses

Through Google OAuth, Forge Shorts only uploads videos and subtitles to channels owned by David Mottet and sets their metadata (title, description, tags, language, publication date).

What it stores

Only the identifiers of the videos it uploaded, on a private home server. It does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data or YouTube data about other people.

Revoking access and deleting data

Access can be revoked at any time at https://security.google.com/settings/security/permissions

After revocation, or on request, the stored video identifiers and the OAuth token are deleted within 7 days.
